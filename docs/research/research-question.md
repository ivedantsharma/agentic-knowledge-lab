# Research Questions — Draft

These are drafts. We will refine them in Phase 1.

## RQ1
Can information architectures designed for LLM agents improve multi-hop knowledge retrieval accuracy and/or reduce context-token consumption compared with standard fixed-size chunk-based RAG?

## RQ2
Can an adaptive retrieval policy choose between lexical, dense, hierarchical, and graph retrieval more effectively than a single static retrieval strategy?

## RQ3
Does supervised or reinforcement-based training improve retrieval-tool selection over prompt-only agent behavior?

## RQ4
Which failure modes appear most frequently in agentic knowledge search under multi-hop, temporal, contradictory, ambiguous, adversarial, and unanswerable tasks?
