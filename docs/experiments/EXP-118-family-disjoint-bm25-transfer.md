# EXP-118 — Family-Disjoint BM25 Transfer Audit

## Research question

Does a tabular REINFORCE retrieval policy trained on one grounded
evidence family transfer to *different* real KnowledgeBench families
when all rankings are generated by the same BM25 retriever?

## Motivation and retriever-confound prevention

EXP-117 proved reward-driven learning on a frozen **hybrid** Cirrus
ranking, where the authoritative manual was ranked second. The
fresh BM25 ranking for the same query ranks that manual first.
An apparent performance change caused by switching retrievers is NOT
evidence of RL generalization or regression.

EXP-118 therefore trains a **new** tabular policy from scratch
on the BM25-generated route-003 ranking. The same BM25 index/ranking
process is used for every evaluation task, ensuring comparability
within this experiment.

## Measured data

- 22 real KnowledgeBench documents.
- 12 real routing questions, two queries per evidence family.
- Training family: Cirrus retry, task route-003 only.
- Same-family diagnostic: route-004 (not counted as heldout).
- Heldout: ten queries from five other families: Borealis,
  Solstice, Meridian, Nimbus, and Orion.
- Split by `pair_id`, not random question rows.
- Supporting labels come from source tasks; no synthetic label flips.

The two queries in each family share supporting evidence. This means
question-level random train/test splits would leak evidence families.

## Hypotheses (pre-registered)

1. REINFORCE can learn a high-return policy on its BM25 Cirrus
   training query.
2. A policy observing only `requested_k` and `steps_taken`
   cannot infer whether the held-out task needs K1, K2, or K3.
3. Measured heldout reward/coverage may therefore diverge from
   training reward, even when the same retrieval engine is used.
4. The gold-aware oracle provides a diagnostic upper bound. Fixed
   K1/K2/K3/K5 policies are important non-learned comparisons.
5. The EXP-117 hybrid-trained results must not be compared directly
   to the EXP-118 BM25-trained results as if the retriever were fixed.

## Protocol

- Use the repository's exact `BM25Retriever` over all 22 documents.
- Verify one task per unique task ID and gold supporting IDs in corpus.
- Recompute full ranking independently for each of the 12 queries.
- Train REINFORCE on BM25 route-003 only: 1600 episodes per seed,
  with seeds 0 through 4, gamma 0.9, default parameters.
- Evaluate initial and final policies on all ten family-disjoint
  questions using 400 independent evaluation episodes per seed/task.
- Report seed-level train, heldout, completion, return differences
  and a per-family heldout breakdown for seed zero.
- Report fixed-policy mean heldout returns/completion and an
  evaluator-only gold-aware oracle ceiling.
- Evaluate policy with heldout gold solely inside
  `RetrievalEpisodeV2` and evaluation metrics, not as policy inputs.
- Measure evidence retrieval only; no answer generation.

## Interpret carefully

- This tests family transfer on **one small real benchmark corpus**,
  not broad domain generalization.
- The policy deliberately does not read the question, scores,
  evidence text, freshness, or contradiction diagnostics, despite
  `RetrievalObservation.question` being visible. Its state key
  remains `(requested_k, steps_taken)`, preventing task-specific
  adaptation. This is an *information bottleneck*.
- A learned fixed preference can transfer well to similarly ranked
  tasks without demonstrating adaptive retrieval intelligence.
- BM25 ranks are NOT the hybrid rankings used in Phase 11 SFT; and
  the Phase 11 classifier also includes a `REFORMULATE` action absent
  from the Phase 12 RL environment. Phase 11 SFT accuracy and Phase 12
  RL reward are thus not a direct apples-to-apples comparison.
- Gold evidence coverage is not a correct final answer; conflict
  resolution and unanswerable tasks remain outside this reward.
- Avoid reporting seed-specific results as representative of the whole
  distribution; disclose all five seeds and across-seed variance.

## Decision gate

If there is a large heldout oracle gap or heterogeneous family
performance, prioritize adding policy-visible *retrieval evidence
signals* rather than increasing model size or switching directly
to PPO/GRPO. Close Phase 12 only after documenting the limited action
space, protocol compatibility and negative generalization results.
